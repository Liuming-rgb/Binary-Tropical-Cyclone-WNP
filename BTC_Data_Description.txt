CSV Field Definitions

Data_BTC.csv

Member 1 and member 2 follow the original cyclone identifier (SID) order. They do not indicate stronger and weaker members. All timestamps are UTC.

Pair_ID: Unique identifier for a cyclone-pair event.
sample_id: Numerical event index.
Year: Event year, based on the start of the first qualifying segment.
Typhoon 1: Cyclone SID of member 1.
Typhoon 2: Cyclone SID of member 2.

Overlap Start: Start of the first qualifying coexistence segment.
Overlap End: End of the last qualifying coexistence segment.
Overlap Duration (hours): Total duration of all qualifying segments, excluding gaps, in hours.
Both Record Start: Earliest common observation time with valid positions for both members.
Both Record End: Last common observation time with valid positions for both members.
Full Path Start: Same as Both Record Start.
Full Path End: Same as Both Record End.
Full Path Elapsed Hours: Elapsed time from Full Path Start to Full Path End, in hours.

Typhoon 1 Time: Observation time of member 1.
Typhoon 2 Time: Observation time of member 2, synchronized with member 1.
Typhoon 1 Latitude: Latitude of member 1, in degrees north.
Typhoon 1 Longitude: Longitude of member 1, in degrees, using the −180 to 180 convention.
Typhoon 1 Intensity: Wind speed of member 1 at the observation time, in knots (kt).
Typhoon 2 Latitude: Latitude of member 2, in degrees north.
Typhoon 2 Longitude: Longitude of member 2, in degrees, using the −180 to 180 convention.
Typhoon 2 Intensity: Wind speed of member 2 at the observation time, in kt.
Spherical Distance (km): Great-circle distance between the two cyclone centers, in km.

CenterClusterK4: Event's common-center trajectory cluster label for K=4, numbered 1–4.
in_qualifying_segment: Whether the observation belongs to a continuous qualifying segment lasting at least 24 elapsed hours.
conditions_met_at_record: Whether both wind speeds are at least 35 kt and center separation is at most 1,800 km at that observation time.
ClusterType: Same as CenterClusterK4.

Typhoon 1 Datetime: Same as Typhoon 1 Time.
Typhoon 2 Datetime: Same as Typhoon 2 Time.
Month: Observation year and month, represented by the first day of the month.
start_time: Same as Overlap Start.
end_time: Same as Overlap End.
both_record_start_time: Same as Both Record Start.
both_record_end_time: Same as Both Record End.
qualifying_start_time: Same as Overlap Start.
qualifying_end_time: Same as Overlap End.
duration_hours: Same as Overlap Duration (hours).
cohort_member_1_max_wind: Maximum wind speed of member 1 over the expanded common observation period, in kt.
cohort_member_2_max_wind: Maximum wind speed of member 2 over the expanded common observation period, in kt.

Environmental fields ending in 1 or 2 refer to member 1 or member 2, respectively.

sst1, sst2: Sea surface temperature, in °C.
air1, air2: Tropopause temperature, in K.
t500_c1, t500_c2: Air temperature at 500 hPa, in °C.
stabily1, stabily2: Stability index defined as 500 hPa air temperature minus sea surface temperature (t500_c − sst), expressed as a temperature difference in °C.
wind_shear1, wind_shear2: Magnitude of the horizontal wind-vector difference between 200 and 850 hPa, in m/s.
mld1, mld2: Ocean mixed-layer depth, in m.
z500_gpm1, z500_gpm2: Geopotential height at 500 hPa, in geopotential meters (gpm).

The following fields average the available values of the two members. If only one member has a valid value, that value is retained.

sst: Mean sea surface temperature of the pair, in °C.
air: Mean tropopause temperature of the pair, in K.
wind_shear: Mean vertical wind shear of the pair, in m/s.
stabily: Mean stability index of the pair, expressed as a temperature difference in °C.
mld: Mean ocean mixed-layer depth of the pair, in m.
t500_c: Mean air temperature at 500 hPa of the pair, in °C.
z500_gpm: Mean geopotential height at 500 hPa of the pair, in gpm.


BTC_Duration.csv

A qualifying segment requires both wind speeds to be at least 35 kt and center separation to be at most 1,800 km at consecutive 6-hour observation times over at least 24 elapsed hours. All timestamps are UTC.

sample_id: Numerical event index, matching Data_BTC.csv.
Pair_ID: Unique cyclone-pair event identifier, matching Data_BTC.csv.
year: Year of qualifying_start.
month: Month of qualifying_start, as a number from 1 to 12.
sid_1: Cyclone SID of member 1.
name_1: Cyclone name of member 1.
sid_2: Cyclone SID of member 2.
name_2: Cyclone name of member 2.
qualifying_start: Start of the first qualifying coexistence segment.
qualifying_end: End of the last qualifying coexistence segment.
qualifying_duration_hours: Total duration of all qualifying segments, excluding gaps, in hours.
qualifying_record_count: Total number of observations in qualifying segments, including each segment's endpoints.
n_qualifying_segments: Number of qualifying continuous segments in the event.
full_start: Start of the original event summary period; equals qualifying_start in this file.
full_end: Last common observation time of the two members.
full_duration_hours: Elapsed time from full_start to full_end, in hours.
full_record_count: Number of observations between full_start and full_end.
maximum_member_1_kt: Maximum wind speed of member 1 between full_start and full_end, in kt.
maximum_member_2_kt: Maximum wind speed of member 2 between full_start and full_end, in kt.
intense: Whether at least one member reaches 115 kt within the original event summary period.
